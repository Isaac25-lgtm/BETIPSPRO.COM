# Market taxonomy (`markets-v1`)

Generated by `scripts/build-market-catalog.mjs` into `shared/market-catalog.json`. **56 market definitions**, all quality-gated per fixture.

| Category | Markets | Tier | Model | Settlement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Match Result | 1X2 (3), Double chance (3) | A | Dixon-Coles scoreline | Regulation time (90' + stoppage); ET/penalties excluded |
| Goals | Over/Under 0.5–4.5 (10) | A | Dixon-Coles | Regulation; own goals count |
| Both Teams to Score | Yes/No (2) | A | Dixon-Coles | Regulation |
| Team Goals | Home/Away over 0.5, 1.5 (4) | A | Dixon-Coles | Regulation |
| Correct Score | 0-0 … 3-3 (16) + Other (1) | A | Dixon-Coles | Regulation; research-grade until calibrated |
| First Half | HT result (3), HT goals O/U 0.5, 1.5 (4) | B | Separate first-half Poisson | Half-time score only |
| Corners | Over/Under 8.5, 9.5, 10.5 (6) | B | Negative binomial (team for/against) | Regulation; operators differ |
| Cards | Yellow cards O/U 3.5, 4.5 (4) | B | Negative binomial (team + referee) | Raw yellow count, **not** booking points |

Each definition has: id, family, category, settlement period, label, selection, threshold, tier, model family, required data, predicate, settlement rules, exclusive group, validation status (`unvalidated` until real-data backtests pass), licensing status (`review_required`), `active_for_beta`.

## Not published (with reasons, shown in the UI)

Player props · referee specials · red cards / penalties · booking points · combinations (need joint simulation) · in-play.

## Bookmaker mapping (API-Football `/odds`)

Only exact mappings: `Match Winner` → 1X2, `Double Chance` → DC, `Both Teams Score` → BTTS, `Goals Over/Under` (0.5–4.5) → totals. Anything else is ignored. **betPawa compatibility is not claimed**; settlement rules must be checked against the operator.
